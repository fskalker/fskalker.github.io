Felix Kalker

I am a PhD student at Leiden University under the supervision of Jan Vonk. I am interested in the interplay between number theory, geometry and analysis. Currently I am trying to find a connection between the evaluations of (rigid) meromorphic cocycles and linking numbers of geodesics in modular curves.

Email: f.s.kalker (at) math.leidenuniv.nl •GitHub
